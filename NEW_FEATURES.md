## 📊 3. Environmental Impact Dashboard

### What's New?
Real-time tracking of your positive environmental impact!

### Metrics Tracked:

| Metric | Icon | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **CO₂ Saved** | 🌫️ | Kilograms of CO₂ emissions prevented |
| **Water Saved** | 💧 | Liters of water conserved |
| **Waste Reduced** | ♻️ | Kilograms of waste diverted from landfills |
| **Trees Equivalent** | 🌳 | Number of trees planted equivalent |

### Calculations:
- **CO₂**: 2.5 kg per completed challenge
- **Water**: 15 liters per completed challenge
- **Waste**: 0.8 kg per completed challenge
- **Trees**: 1 tree per 4 challenges

### Features:
- 📈 Animated stat counters
- 🎨 Color-coded cards with gradients
- 📊 Real-time updates
- 💫 Smooth number animations
